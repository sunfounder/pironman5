2. Assembly Instructions
=============================================

Before assembling the Pironman 5 Ultra, please first verify that all parts and components have been included. If there are any missing or damaged components, please contact SunFounder immediately at service@sunfounder.com to resolve the issue as soon as possible.

**Tips**

* It is recommended to follow the printed installation instructions step by step.
* Before installation, sort and stack the components to find them more quickly.
* Repeatedly check the connections of FFC and FPC cables to avoid poor contact.
* Peel off the protective film from the acrylic panels before assembly, and remove the lens protection sticker from the camera opening if a camera module is used.

**Battery Installation**

The **Power Pack (SFB20003C2S)** battery is installed on the side acrylic panel. Connect it to the PiPower 5 module with the VH3.96-2P connector.

.. warning::

   Make sure the battery connector is inserted with the correct polarity. Do not reverse it, do not short-circuit the battery, and do not pierce or crush the battery pack.
