CPU Cooler
===============

Pironman 5 Ultra cools the Raspberry Pi 5 with an **aluminum heatsink** combined with a **3010 blower (turbo) fan**. The fan is driven by the Raspberry Pi 5 fan connector and follows the standard Raspberry Pi 5 temperature curve.

.. note::

   The turbo fan only has a control signal and no tachometer (speed) signal, so the fan speed cannot be read back by the system. This is normal and does not indicate a fault. The Temperature card on the Dashboard may show ``0 RPM`` for the CPU fan.

**Warning**

* Do not touch the blades, and do not let the power wires wrap around the fan.
* When the fan is working, do not try to lock the fan for a long time. Otherwise the fan may burn out due to the heat generated by the continuous stoppage.
* When assembling the cooler, pay special attention to the noise generated by resonance or vibration.

**Fan Speed Control**

See :ref:`fan_ultra` for the PWM fan gears and the default speed curve.
